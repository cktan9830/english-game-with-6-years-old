Family code

Bahasa Melayu spelling words (ejaan)

This week's list, one word per line. Add "= meaning" for the English meaning, e.g. "pokok = tree". Type é for an e said as in merah (e.g. "mérah = red"); a plain e is the soft e of emak. Words that weren't recorded are read by the tablet's own Malay voice.

Voice for the Malay words

Bunyi huruf (Malay letter sounds)

Bahasa Melayu is read with its own sounds, not English ones, and Eja! uses these: a is always "a" as in ayam; e is usually the soft pepet of emak and sometimes the é of merah (taling); i, o and u are pure; c is "ch"; g is always hard; r is rolled; a final h is breathed; p, t and k have no puff of air; ng, ny, sy and kh are one sound each. Tap a sound to hear it, then a word with it.

Letter sounds (English)

Tap a letter to hear the phonics sound Luna uses, then a word with it. British English, as taught in school: "o" as in "on", "a" as in "ant".

How to play

    This browser can't speak words out loud, so Luna's voice is off. Each round, the word appears on your side for you to say, then her cards appear.

    Check the Malay sounds

    Every Malay recording in the game, for a grown-up to check before she hears it. Tap one to hear it (a tick shows you've heard it), or play a whole group. If a sound is not right, tap its ✗ to mark it. Then copy the list and send it, and those recordings will be re-made. A mark stays until that recording changes.
